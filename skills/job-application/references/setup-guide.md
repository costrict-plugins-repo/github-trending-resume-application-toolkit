# Setup Guide — Job Application Toolkit

Welcome. This guide walks you through setting up the Job Application Toolkit for first-time use. You only need to do this once. After setup, you simply paste job ads and the toolkit handles the rest.

## What You Need Before Starting

Gather the following before you begin:

1. **Your career history** — employer names, dates, and what you did at each
2. **3-8 key project achievements** — with specific, measurable outcomes (numbers, percentages, dollar amounts)
3. **Your qualifications and certifications** — with completion years
4. **One well-written cover letter** — real or drafted — that represents your best professional voice. This becomes your "gold standard" template.

If you do not have a cover letter you are happy with, the setup process can help you draft one.

## Step 1: Copy the Instructions Template

The instructions template is the only file you need to fill in. Copy it to your workspace:

```
Copy instructions-template.md to your workspace folder as INSTRUCTIONS.md
```

Claude can do this for you. Just say: "Help me set up my job application instructions."

## Step 2: Fill In Your Profile

Open INSTRUCTIONS.md and work through each section marked [FILL IN]. Here is what goes in each:

### Who I Am

Write 3-5 sentences as if you were introducing yourself to a recruiter over coffee. Include:
- Your name and location
- Current role and employer
- Total years of experience and which sectors/industries
- What you are looking for next (be honest — transitions are fine)
- Any distinctive background (military, career change, unique qualification)

**What good looks like:**

> Sam Chen. Sydney, Australia. Australian citizen.
>
> Currently at Deloitte (March 2022 - Present) as a Senior Consultant in the Technology and Transformation practice. Before that, Accenture (2018-2022) and two years at a mid-size digital agency.
>
> 8 years in technology delivery across financial services, government, and retail. Certified Scrum Master and AWS Solutions Architect. Strong track record in data platform delivery and Agile coaching.
>
> I am looking to move from consulting into an embedded delivery role. I want to build something rather than advise on it. Government or purpose-driven organisations are my preference. This is not a step down — it is a deliberate choice to apply my depth of experience in a setting where I can see the long-term impact.

### Employer and Client Mappings

List every relevant employer with dates and the clients or projects you worked on. This is especially important for consultants who delivered work for external clients — it lets Claude correctly attribute projects.

### Achievements Bank

This is the raw material Claude draws from. For each achievement, write 2-4 sentences covering:
- What the project or initiative was
- What your specific role was
- How you approached it
- What the measurable result was

**What good looks like:**

> Enterprise data platform for ANZ Bank — 18-month delivery across three workstreams. I ran the delivery cadence, facilitated requirements workshops with Marketing and Risk, and managed scope against a fixed timeline. Designed and embedded an Agile way of working that gave the team a sustainable rhythm. Result: platform delivered on time, $15k/month reduction in manual reporting costs.

**What weak looks like:**

> Delivered a data platform for ANZ. Successfully managed stakeholders and delivered on time.

The weak version gives Claude nothing to work with. The strong version has specifics — duration, scope, approach, metric.

### Professional Summary

Write a 4-paragraph base version. Paragraphs 1 and 3 will be adapted per role. Paragraphs 2 and 4 stay close to base. See the template for the structure.

### Core Skills

Write 6-8 competency lines. Each should be one sentence in "Label: Description" format. These get reordered per role, so write them to stand alone.

### Gold Standard Cover Letter

This is the most important piece. Write one complete cover letter following the 5-paragraph pattern. Claude will start from this text for every application, adapting the marked sections.

If you are struggling with this, paste a job ad that represents your target market and say: "Help me write my gold standard cover letter for this type of role." Claude can draft it collaboratively with you.

### Writing Style

Describe your voice. Read the banned phrases list and add any phrases that are overused in your industry or that do not sound like you. The tone description is particularly important — it helps Claude match your authentic voice rather than producing generic professional language.

## Step 3: Verify Your Setup

Once INSTRUCTIONS.md is complete, say: "Verify my instructions setup." Claude will read the file and check:

- All [FILL IN] sections have been populated
- Employer dates are consistent with the mappings table
- Achievement metrics are specific and measurable
- The gold standard cover letter follows the 5-paragraph pattern
- Writing style section has tone described

Claude will flag anything that needs attention.

## Step 4: Start Applying

Paste a job ad and say "new application" or "help me apply for this." The toolkit takes over from there.

## Tips for Best Results

**More achievements are better.** The toolkit selects the most relevant ones per application. If you only have three, every application will draw from the same pool. Eight to ten gives Claude meaningful choices.

**Update your instructions as your career evolves.** New project? New certification? Add it. The instructions file is a living document.

**Review the first 2-3 applications carefully.** Check whether the tone matches your voice, the keyword placements feel natural, and the cover letter reads like something you would actually send. Provide feedback — Claude will adjust.

**Keep a job tracker.** The toolkit can maintain a spreadsheet tracking every application. Create a blank .xlsx file in your workspace and the toolkit will populate it.
