Privacy Policy

Job Application Toolkit — Last updated 31 March 2026

Overview

The Job Application Toolkit is a locally-run plugin for Claude Code and Cowork. It is designed to process your career information and job advertisements entirely on your own device. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data Collection

The Job Application Toolkit does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking cookies, and no external network calls made by the plugin itself.

Where Your Data Lives

All data you provide to the toolkit — your career history, achievements, employer details, and generated documents — is stored locally on your device in the workspace folder you select. The plugin does not send this information anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The plugin runs within Claude Code or Cowork, which are products of Anthropic. When the plugin generates documents, it uses Claude (the AI model) to process your instructions and job advertisements. Your interactions with Claude are governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

The plugin itself does not independently connect to any third-party services, APIs, or external servers.

Generated Documents

Resumes, cover letters, keyword maps, and other documents generated by the toolkit are saved to your local file system. They are not uploaded, cached, or stored anywhere other than the folders you designate on your device.

Open Source

The plugin's source code is publicly available on GitHub. You can inspect exactly what the plugin does at any time.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Since the plugin runs locally and collects no data, meaningful changes to this policy are unlikely.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach the maintainer via the GitHub Issues page.
